## Proposed text

**Remembering the Waters** is Kenya's first national archive dedicated to maritime and inland-water safety heritage. Its full title is *Remembering the Waters: Co-creating Kenya's Maritime and Inland-Water Safety Heritage Archive for Equity, Memory, and Future Transitions*.

It documents past ferry and boat disasters, everyday safety experiences and community knowledge from the coast, the lakes and the rivers, so that lives lost and lessons learned are not forgotten, and so that this knowledge informs safer water transport in future.
