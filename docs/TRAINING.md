# Training sessions: trainer's plan

Plans for the five sessions in [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md). Each is a half-day or less, taught on the **test site** (https://maritime-heritage.onrender.com), where nothing done affects the real archive. The first visit after a quiet period takes about a minute to wake the site: open it before the session starts.

**For every session:** a projector; one phone or computer per two people; internet; the attendance register; and the short questions at the start and end, which give the pre- and post-training feedback for the Training Evaluation Report.

**Test accounts:** participants sign in with the test sign-in (any name). The trainer uses the admin and Super Admin test accounts in `ACCESS-CREDENTIALS.txt`, which must not be shared outside the team.

## Session 1: Using the archive (everyone, 1.5 hours)

Guide: [USER-GUIDE.md](USER-GUIDE.md)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 10 min | What the archive is and who it is for | Trainer shows the STORIES page |
| 20 min | Reading, listening and watching; the notice before distressing stories; transcripts and Translate | Everyone opens three stories |
| 20 min | Finding things: the “Stories by” tabs, Search, the Map, the Timeline, Incidents, Collections | Exercise 1 |
| 15 min | Citing, sharing a link, Favorites, downloading where allowed | Exercise 2 |
| 15 min | Settings: language, text size, theme, low-data mode; reading offline; installing the app | Everyone switches to Kiswahili and back |
| 10 min | Questions; end-of-session questions | |

- **Exercise 1:** find the incident record for the Mtongwe ferry disaster, and say how many items are linked to it.
- **Exercise 2:** copy the citation of one story and paste it into a message to your neighbour.
- **They can do it when:** each person finds a named story three different ways, and can say where the emergency numbers are shown.

## Session 2: Documenting and uploading (researchers, partners, community champions, 3 hours)

Guides: [HANDBOOK.md](HANDBOOK.md), [METADATA.md](METADATA.md)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | Consent: explaining the archive, naming or anonymity, downloads, withdrawal, children, validation with the storyteller | Role play in pairs with the consent wording |
| 20 min | Stories about loss: pace, what not to photograph, other people's details, recent incidents | Discussion |
| 30 min | Recording well with a phone: sound, light, length, file names | Exercise 1 |
| 20 min | What to write down for every item | Fill one row of the metadata template |
| 40 min | Sharing a story: whose story, voice or files, story details, options, drafts | Exercise 2 |
| 20 min | What happens after SEND: the automatic check, what RED and YELLOW mean, appeals | Trainer shows a held story |
| 20 min | Questions; end-of-session questions | |

- **Exercise 1:** record a one-minute interview with your neighbour, with spoken consent at the start; play back the first ten seconds.
- **Exercise 2:** share that recording on the test site as told by another person, anonymously, marked for the right place and category.
- **They can do it when:** each pair has published one story with consent recorded, and one correct row in the template.

## Session 3: Moderating (admins, 3 hours)

Guide: [ADMIN-GUIDE.md](ADMIN-GUIDE.md)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 20 min | Signing in with the second step; setting up an authenticator app | Everyone sets one up |
| 30 min | How stories are checked: GREEN, YELLOW, RED; the response times | Trainer walks through the queue |
| 45 min | Moderating: approve, hide, remove with a reason; overriding the AI and why it needs approval | Exercise 1 |
| 30 min | Reports and safety concerns; when to call the emergency services or the authorities | Exercise 2 |
| 30 min | Account actions, appeals, and approvals by two-thirds of the admins | Exercise 3 |
| 25 min | The audit log: everything is recorded. Questions; end-of-session questions | |

- **Exercise 1:** the trainer posts three stories (one fine, one with a phone number, one insulting); each admin decides and writes the reason.
- **Exercise 2:** a participant reports a comment; an admin handles the report from start to finish.
- **Exercise 3:** one admin proposes an override; the others vote.
- **They can do it when:** every admin has taken one decision of each kind with a written reason, and can find it in the audit log.

## Session 4: Curating (admins, 3 hours)

Guide: [ADMIN-GUIDE.md](ADMIN-GUIDE.md)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 40 min | Company stories and testimonies: consent type, validation with the storyteller, historical records and their source | Exercise 1 |

| 45 min | Bulk import: the template, the files, checking, correcting, approval | Exercise 2 |
| 25 min | Collections and online exhibitions; learning resources | Build a three-item collection |

- **Exercise 1:** add one testimony with signed consent and validation, and one historical record with its source.
- **Exercise 2:** import five rows from the template, one with a deliberate mistake; correct it and submit the batch.
- **They can do it when:** each admin has an approved import batch and a published collection on the test site.

## Session 5: Running the archive (Super Admins and the maintainer, 2.5 hours)

Guides: [SUPER-ADMIN-GUIDE.md](SUPER-ADMIN-GUIDE.md), [MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md), [INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md)

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | Managing admins: approve a registration, invite, remove, make a Super Admin; always two Super Admins | Exercise 1 |
| 20 min | Overdue approvals | Decide one |
| 30 min | Live analytics, AI quality, recalibration, PDF reports | Generate a report |
| 20 min | The audit log; System: Background work and Performance | Read both together |
| 30 min | Backups and the restore test; the maintenance calendar and the quarterly review | Walk through the checklist |
| 20 min | An incident practice: “an admin's account has been taken over” | Table-top, using the incident plan |

- **Exercise 1:** a participant registers as an admin; a Super Admin checks and approves them, then removes them.
- **They can do it when:** each Super Admin has approved and removed an admin, generated a report, and can name the first three steps of the incident plan.

## Questions for the start and end of each session

Asked on paper or by show of hands, before and after, on a scale of 1 (not at all) to 5 (fully):

1. I can find a story in the archive about a place or an incident I choose.
2. I can explain to a storyteller what consent means for the archive.
3. I can add a story or material to the archive myself.
4. I know what happens to a story after it is sent, and who decides.
5. *(Admins and Super Admins)* I can carry out my duties on the platform without help.

At the end, also ask: what was least clear, and what should be changed on the platform. Enter those answers in the archive's feedback form so they are kept.
