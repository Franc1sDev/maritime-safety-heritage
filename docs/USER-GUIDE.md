# User guide

For everyone who uses the Maritime Safety Heritage Archive: researchers, communities, educators, policymakers and the public. The website and the Android and iOS app work the same way, with the same account.

## Your account

- **Create an account.** On the website choose **Sign in**, then **Create account**. In the app, tap **Create account**. Use 8 or more characters with at least one letter and one number for your password.
- **Sign in.** Use the same email and password on the website and the app.
- **Forgot your password?** Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. A link arrives by email and works for 30 minutes.
- **After 5 wrong passwords** your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or reset your password.
- **My account** lets you change your name, organisation or password, see your submissions, sign out on every device, or close your account.

## Finding material

- **Search** looks through titles, summaries, descriptions, places and transcripts. Matching words are highlighted. On the website, press **Ctrl + K** from any page for quick search.
- **Browse** lists everything. Use the round buttons to pick a type (oral histories, photographs, records, testimonies), and **Filters** to narrow by water body, decade, county or order.
- **Incident map** shows where each story happened. Tap a marker to open the item, or use **List** to jump to a place.

## Reading, listening and watching

Each item page shows its catalogue details: identifier (for example MSH-0001), date, place, who told or made it, language, rights and consent.

- **Oral histories:** press **Play**. The transcript follows along; tap a line to jump to that moment.
- **Videos** play in the page and can go full screen.
- **Photographs** carry the archive's credit.
- **Documents** open as a PDF.
- **Cite** gives a ready-made citation with the item's identifier. Please use it whenever you quote or refer to an item.
- **Location** shows the place on the map. **Related** suggests similar items.

## Sharing a story or material

Choose **Share a story** on the website or **Share** in the app.

1. Pick what you are sharing: an oral history, photograph, record or testimony.
2. Give it a title and describe it: who, what, when and where.
3. Add up to 5 files: audio, video, photos, PDF or plain text, up to 500 MB each. In the app you can take a photo or record a video straight away.
4. Confirm you have the right to share it and the consent of the people in it.
5. Send it.

The research team reviews everything before it is published. You can follow its status under **My account, My submissions**, and you get an email when it is accepted or declined.

## Saving data and making text easier to read

Open **See more** (website) or the menu (app):

- **Light mode** hides background video and pictures to save data on slow connections. It is remembered on that device.
- **Larger text** makes all text bigger.

## Privacy and closing your account

Read how your information is used in **Privacy**, and the rules for reusing material in **Terms and copyright**. Both are linked at the bottom of every page and in the app menu.

To close your account, go to **My account, Close account** and enter your password. This can't be undone. Your name, email and organisation are erased. Submissions still waiting for review are withdrawn. Material already accepted into the archive stays in it.

## Getting help

Use **Contact the team**. It works without signing in.
