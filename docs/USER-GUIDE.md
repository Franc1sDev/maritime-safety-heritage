# User guide

How to use **Remembering the Waters**, the digital archive of Kenya's maritime and inland-water safety heritage, on the website and in the Android and iOS apps. The two look and work the same; where the app differs, this guide says so.

For people collecting material for the project, see also the [Handbook](HANDBOOK.md). For the project team, see the [Admin manual](ADMIN-GUIDE.md).

> **In an emergency on the water, call 999 or 112.** The archive is not an emergency service.

## Who can do what

| You are | You can |
|---|---|
| **A visitor** (not signed in) | Browse, search, read, listen, watch, share links, copy citations, read the learning resources, report a safety concern, and send feedback |
| **A Contributor** (signed in with Google or Apple) | Everything a visitor can, plus share stories, react, comment, save Favorites, follow people, send messages, report content, and set reminders |

Reading the archive is always free and never needs an account.

## The main screen

The archive opens on **STORIES**, one story at a time. Round icons at the top lead to everything else:

- **STORIES**: the archive itself.
- **ANNOUNCEMENTS**: news from the project team, with a calendar of dates.
- **INBOX**: your messages (signed in).
- **MY PROFILE**: your stories, drafts, reminders, notifications and settings (signed in), or **SIGN IN**.
- **ABOUT & RESOURCES**: about the project, the rules, learning resources, the safety concern form and the feedback form.

When you open one of these, the stories stay in view behind it. Select them to go back.

The first time you open the archive, four short hints show how to browse, react, comment and share. You can see them again from **Settings › Hints**.

## Finding stories

**Moving between stories.** Scroll, swipe up, or use the arrow keys for the next story. Swipe or scroll back for the previous one.

**“Stories by” tabs.** Choose how to browse:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Trending** | The stories people are engaging with most now |
| **Following** | Stories from Contributors you follow (signed in) |
| **Location** | Stories from a place you type or choose |
| **Contributors** | One Contributor's stories; also **Find Contributors** |
| **Favorites** | Stories you saved (signed in) |
| **Incidents** | Incident records, such as the Mtongwe ferry disaster of 1994, each with its linked stories. You can download the incident list as a spreadsheet (CSV) |
| **Map** | **Map Explore**: blue markers are stories, red markers are incident records. Narrow the map by category, language, and the dates between which the events happened |
| **Timeline** | Stories by the decade and year in which the events happened |
| **Collections** | Collections and online exhibitions put together by the project team |

**Search.** Type words in **Search the archive**. If nothing matches exactly, **Also found** lists near matches.

## Reading, listening and watching

Each story shows who shared it, when and where it happened, its rights and source, and its text, photos, audio, video or documents.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Read the whole story** | Opens the full text |
| **Listen** | Reads the story aloud, if your device has a voice for that language |
| **Transcript** | Shows the words of an audio or video recording |
| **Translate** | Shows a machine translation of the story, or of a comment, in your language. It is marked as machine-translated, and **Show original** goes back. (Translation works only when the project has turned the service on.) |
| **Where It Happened** | Opens the place on a map |
| **Edit history** | Shows that a story was edited, and when |

A story that describes loss of life shows a notice first: *This story describes loss of life and may be distressing.* You choose whether to open it.

Stories told to the project by someone else are marked **as told by** that person. A storyteller who asked not to be named is shown as anonymous.

## Reacting, commenting and saving (signed in)

- **React** with a heart, a heartbreak or a laugh. Select it again to take it back.
- **Comments**: write a comment or reply to one. Type `@` to tag someone. After you post, your comment is shown first. Comments can be sorted by most reactions, most recent or oldest.
- **Favorite** (☆): saves the story to your **Favorites** tab. Select it again to remove it.
- **Follow** a Contributor from their profile to see their stories in **Following**. From a profile you can also **Message**, **Mute** or **Block** the person.

Every comment is checked automatically before it appears. If one is held for review, you are told.

## Sharing a link, citing and downloading

- **Share**: send the story's link through another app, or **Copy Link**. Links show a preview in WhatsApp and on social media.
- **Cite**: copies a suggested citation with the story's permanent link, for research and teaching.
- **Download**: saves the story's files, where the storyteller has allowed downloads.

Please respect the **Rights** shown on each story when you reuse it.

## Signing in

1. Select **SIGN IN**, then **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. The archive never sees your password.
2. The first time, set up your profile: a username (3 to 30 letters, numbers or underscores), a picture, your language and theme. Confirm that you are 18 or older, and accept the terms and the privacy policy.
3. A short welcome shows the rules and how stories are checked.

You cannot use “Admin” as a username or the project logo as your picture.

## Sharing your own story

Select the **+** on the STORIES page (**Share my story**).

1. **Whose story is this?** Your own, or **as told by another person**. For someone else's story you must confirm that they agreed to it being shared; for anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must have agreed.
2. **Your story.** Type it, or **tell it by voice or add files**. A story needs at least one thing: text, a recording or a file.
3. **Files.** Up to 20 images (25 MB each), 4 documents (25 MB each), 1 audio file (100 MB) and 1 video (200 MB, 5 minutes). You can record audio or video on the spot. Photos are made smaller before they are sent, and you can choose **Blur faces in the photos I add next**: the phone finds the faces and blurs them before the photo is sent (in the app, and on the website where the browser can find faces). If the phone cannot look for faces, the photo is not added and you are told.
4. **Story details.** Category and tags (or **Suggest category and tags**), the language, **when it happened** (a date, or just a year if you only know roughly), **where it happened** (type a place or put a pin on the map), a vessel name, and a **linked incident** if it belongs to one. If the incident is not listed you can suggest it to the admins.
5. **Options.** Post under your name or **anonymously** (the admins can still see who posted). Choose the usage rights, whether others may download it, and whether to mark it as **describing loss of life or possibly distressing**.
6. **SEND**, or **Save draft** to finish later from **MY PROFILE › Drafts**.

Large files are sent in pieces and continue where they stopped if the connection drops. You can **Pause** and **Resume** them. In the app you can press SEND while files are still uploading: the story is saved and sent when they arrive, and “Upload only on Wi-Fi” in Settings is respected.

### What happens after you send

Every story is checked automatically against the rules, usually in seconds and within 3 minutes.

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Your story is live |
| **Published with a note** | It is live, with a private note to you about what to avoid next time |
| **Blocked** | It is not published. You are told why, and the admins review it. Use **Ask Why** to question the decision |

You can edit or delete your stories from **MY PROFILE › My Stories**. Changes to a published story are checked before they show.

## Messages (INBOX)

- **Message the admins** with a question. They reply in your INBOX.
- Message another Contributor from their profile. A first message from someone you do not follow arrives as a **request**: **Accept** to reply or **Decline** to remove it.
- **Block** someone to stop them messaging you or interacting with your stories. Unblock them in Settings.
- Messages are checked automatically before they are delivered.

## Reporting

- **Report** on a story, a comment or a person: pick one or more reasons, and add a description if you wish. The admins review reports; urgent ones (a safety risk, or private data exposed) first.
- **Report a safety concern** (ABOUT & RESOURCES) to tell the project about a current risk on the water, with a place and an optional photo. You do not need an account. Your identity is not published.

## MY PROFILE

| Section | What is there |
|---|---|
| **My Stories** | Your stories with their views, reactions, comments and shares; edit or delete them |
| **Drafts** | Unfinished stories |
| **Reminders** | Notes to yourself with a date and time |
| **Notifications** | Reactions, comments, mentions, follows, messages and announcements; items that need your action stay at the top |
| **Settings** | Language, theme (light, dark or automatic), text size, high contrast, reduce motion, low-data mode, read receipts, quiet hours, push notifications, blocked and muted people, and the hints |
| **Download my data** | A ZIP file with all your stories, comments and profile data |
| **Delete account** | See below |

## Weak or no connection

- A banner shows when you are offline. Stories you already opened can be read again.
- Reactions, comments and messages made offline wait and are sent when the connection returns.
- A story you are writing is kept on your device.
- **Low-data mode** loads pictures and video only when you select them.

## Languages and accessibility

- Change the language at any time from the language menu or Settings. English and Kiswahili are complete; Kiswahili is awaiting review by a native-speaker reviewer. Other languages are planned.
- The archive works with the keyboard and screen readers, in light and dark themes, with larger text and high contrast, and with reduced motion.

## Your privacy and closing your account

- The **Privacy policy** and **Terms of use** are in ABOUT & RESOURCES.
- **Delete account**: choose a reason (and add your own words if you wish). Your account and content are removed at once and permanently erased after 30 days. To restore the account, sign in again within 30 days.

## Getting help

- **Message the admins** from your INBOX.
- Use the **Feedback form** in ABOUT & RESOURCES; no account is needed.
- Read **Rules and netiquette** and **How stories are checked** in ABOUT & RESOURCES.
