## Press notice (English)

**Remembering the Waters: Kenya's first archive of maritime and inland-water safety heritage opens to the public**

[Place], [date]. A free public archive of the stories, testimonies and records of safety on Kenya's ocean, lakes and rivers opens today at [address], and as an app for Android and iPhone.

*Remembering the Waters* brings together the experience of the people who live and work on the water: fishers, ferry crews and passengers, boat operators, rescuers, and the families and communities of the Indian Ocean coast and Lake Victoria. It holds community stories, recorded oral testimonies, historical records, photographs and mapped incident records, among them the ferry and boat tragedies that communities still remember.

“[Quote from the Project Manager: why this memory matters and what the archive makes possible.]”, said [name and title].

Anyone can read, listen and watch without an account, in English or Kiswahili, on a slow connection or offline. People who sign in can share their own story in writing, by voice or on video, by name or anonymously. Every story is checked before it is published, and every testimony is published only with the storyteller's consent.

The archive is also a working resource. Each item has a permanent link and a suggested citation; incidents can be browsed on a map and a timeline; and a learning area holds policy briefs, learning summaries and online exhibitions for safety agencies, researchers, teachers and students.

The archive was built by [Zetech University — confirm] with [partners], with support from [funder — confirm name and wording]. It was created with the communities whose stories it holds, through interviews, community meetings and validation sessions in [counties].

“[Quote from a community representative or partner.]”, said [name, role].

**To take part:** visit [address], or search for “Remembering the Waters” in Google Play or the App Store. In an emergency on the water, call 999 or 112; the archive is not an emergency service.

**Contact:** [name, role, email, phone]

## Announcement on the archive (English)

**Welcome to Remembering the Waters**

The archive is now open. Read, listen to and watch stories of safety on Kenya's waters, browse incidents on the map and the timeline, and share your own story with the **+** button on the STORIES page. Every story is checked before it appears. Read the **Rules and netiquette** in ABOUT & RESOURCES, and tell us what to improve with the feedback form there.
